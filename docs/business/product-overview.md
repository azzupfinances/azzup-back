# Product Overview

## Purpose
Defines what Azzup is and what it must achieve.

## Product
Azzup is a personal finance management application for Brazilian employees working under the CLT employment model. It helps individuals manage income, expenses, bills, and salary cycles. Each user manages their own finances independently.

## Core Questions
The product must answer:
1. How much money came in and went out this month? ([Monthly Dashboard](monthly-dashboard.md))
2. How much money is available until the next salary payment? ([Salary Cycles](salary-cycles.md))
3. Which bills are approaching their due dates, and which are overdue? ([Bills](bills.md))

## Principles
- Simplicity, clarity, and usability.
- Personal finance only. Not corporate accounting or multi-user financial administration.

## Language
All project content is in English ([Language](../conventions/language.md)). Brazilian legal identifiers (CPF, CLT, BRL, 13th salary) keep their recognized names; explanations and technical naming are in English. See [Business Glossary](business-glossary.md).

## Planned Business Areas
Conceptual areas only. Module boundaries and names are decided during implementation; nothing is created yet.
- Authentication and accounts
- User profiles
- Subscriptions
- Transactions
- Statement imports
- Bills
- Salary cycles
- Financial dashboards
- Internal administration
